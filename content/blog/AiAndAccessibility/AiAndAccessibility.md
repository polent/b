## Introduction

Accessibility means making websites and apps easy to use for everyone. It's about designing our digital tools so that all people, including those with different abilities, can use them easily.

{% image "./AIACC1.png", "A modern digital workplace with a diverse group of people using various accessible technologies. This includes people with disabilities using assistive devices and others interacting with AI-powered tools.", [], "(min-width: 40em) 960px, 100vw" %}

Accessibility is important. It's not just about following rules. It's about giving everyone the same chance to use digital platforms, like websites and apps. This means people with disabilities can also gain knowledge, work, and connect with others online.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) helps in this area. It makes it easier to check if our digital tools are accessible. It also improves how they work for people with disabilities. AI can predict what users need and learn over time to get better at helping us.

Next, we look at the current state of accessibility, how AI helps to test and improve it, and what we might see in the future.

## The Current State of Accessibility in Digital Workplaces

Right now, making our digital tools accessible is a big challenge. Many people don't know enough about it or don't see it as a priority. So some websites and apps are hard for people with disabilities to use.

For example, a website might not work well with a screen reader. A screen reader is a tool that reads out the text for people who can't see the screen. Or a site might be hard to navigate if you can't use a mouse.

Following guidelines from groups like the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) is important. They tell us how to make websites that everyone can use.

## The Role of AI in Accessibility Testing

AI is changing the way we check if digital tools are accessible. It's faster than doing it by hand. AI can scan a website and spot problems, like not enough contrast between the text and the background.

There are AI tools that help find and fix these problems. So we can make websites and apps that are easier for everyone to use.

Some companies already use AI for this. For example, Microsoft and Google have projects that use AI to make the web more accessible.

## AI in Day-to-Day Implementation of Accessibility

AI also helps every day to make digital tools more accessible. It can change how a website looks based on what a user needs. This could mean bigger text or different colors, so it's easier to read.

AI helps to create content that is easy to understand for everyone. It can suggest ways to write or design things better.

There are also design tools that use AI to make sure new websites and apps are [accessible from the start](/blog/ContinuousExperiencePipelinesCase2/). This helps to keep up with the latest guidelines.

## The Future of Accessibility with AI Integration

Looking ahead, AI could do even more for accessibility. It might adapt digital tools to fit each person's needs. Then everyone could take part on the web equally.

But we have to keep improving AI tools and [use them in the right way](/blog/WhyUncheckedTrustInAIPutsCriticalThinkingAtRisk/). This makes sure they help everyone and don't leave anyone out.

AI could help us create a digital world that is open to everyone.
